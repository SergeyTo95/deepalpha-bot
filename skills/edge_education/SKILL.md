# Edge education

## Purpose
Explain edge in Polymarket in a short, practical, Russian-first way.

## Teaching points
- edge = разница между рыночной ценой и независимой оценкой вероятности.
- Example: if a YES price implies 51%, but independent analysis estimates 60%, there may be potential edge before costs.
- Always check market resolution rules: the exact wording can change the real probability.
- Check liquidity and spread: a good theoretical edge can disappear on entry or exit.
- Check news timing: ask whether the news is fresh or already priced in.
- Check whether the market has already absorbed the obvious information.
- Small edge can disappear after fees, slippage, spread, or poor fill quality.
- Do not give a final recommendation without full market context.

## Output style
Short, practical, Russian-first. Prefer examples with percentages and a reminder that edge is not the same as certainty.
