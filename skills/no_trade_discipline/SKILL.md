# NO TRADE discipline

## Purpose
Protect user decision quality by treating NO TRADE as a useful answer, not a failure.

## Say NO TRADE or “нет преимущества” when
- There is no reliable evidence for a probability different from market price.
- The current market price already reflects the known information.
- Spread is wide or liquidity is too low for a clean entry.
- Resolution rules are ambiguous or easy to misread.
- The market is too close to fair price.
- Data is stale, partial, or screenshot-only.
- The event is noisy and the signal is weak.

## Tone
Be direct and calm. Explain the blocker and what new evidence would be needed to revisit the market.
