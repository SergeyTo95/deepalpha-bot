Privacy Policy

Velia is an AI assistant from Velyon Core. This policy explains what information the service processes, including data you choose to connect from Google, and how you can control it.

Effective date: November 12, 2026 · Contact: support@velyoncore.ai

1. Who operates Velia

Velia is provided under the Velyon Core project name (referred to as “Velia,” “we,” or “us”). Questions about personal information, access, corrections, or deletion can be directed to support@velyoncore.ai.

2. Information we process

  • Account information: identifiers and profile information needed to sign you in, maintain your session, and associate your conversations with your account. Login may use Telegram verification.
  • Content you provide: chat messages, prompts, attachments, voice input/transcriptions, documents, and requests you submit to the assistant.
  • Missions: user-directed plans, checkpoints, source excerpts, limited context from the originating chat, approval records, completion notices and generated files are stored encrypted in the existing account database until you delete the mission in Tasks. Mission execution may decrypt relevant saved notebook records on the server and send selected excerpts to your chosen AI model to prepare drafts. Generated files are limited to 512 KB each. Deleting a mission removes its checkpoints and files; it does not remove outputs already saved to Notes, account conversations, Gmail or Calendar. Complex text requests may be queued for background execution with the model you selected. Results are restored in the originating chat. Task notices are saved with the mission and forwarded through the existing Pulse delivery pipeline as one-off notices, respecting notification consent, previews and quiet hours. A delivery failure does not delete the result. Deleting a chat detaches its missions from chat restoration; delete the missions separately in Tasks to remove their saved results. Calendar creation and Gmail drafts require confirmation of the exact action. The current mission tools do not send email or transfer money.
  • Interactive apps: requested calculators, tables and charts use a restricted numeric definition. Values are recalculated in your browser. App definitions and edits may remain in your encrypted device chat history; saving to Notes syncs them through the encrypted notebook. Creating a custom definition or discussing it sends relevant text and values to the selected AI model with web search disabled.
  • Saved memory: facts and decisions you explicitly save are stored in the existing encrypted notebook, along with source excerpts and links to projects, people or topics you specify. When you ask about saved memory, relevant excerpts are sent to your selected AI model to select supporting quotations. You can inspect, edit, disable or delete records in Memory settings. Deleting a memory record does not delete its original source message or past chat replies. Memory and notes share the existing notebook storage limits.
  • Road routes: when you request a route, its coordinates and travel mode may be sent to openrouteservice by HeiGIT through our server. Contact names, meeting details, account identifiers and chat text are not included in that provider request. A short-lived route cache is held in server memory for up to ten minutes, and route results may remain in your encrypted device chat history. Estimated travel times exclude live traffic and time at stops. Opening an external navigator transfers the coordinates in its link to that service.
  • Meeting information: when you request meeting analysis, the supplied transcript is processed by your selected AI model. Audio uploads are sent to the configured speech recognition service when available; temporary server files used to check audio duration are deleted after processing. The original audio is not retained by the gateway. Transcripts and draft protocols are kept in your encrypted device chat history; saving protocols to Notes or tasks to CRM requires your separate action. Quotes support review but do not guarantee transcription or interpretation accuracy. This feature does not identify voices, record meetings in the background, send messages, or join meetings.
  • CRM information: contact names and details, roles, companies, recorded communication history, deals, commitments, follow-up dates and links you provide. At your explicit request, Velia can read Gmail for a selected contact and period using your connected Google account, then save message subjects, snippets, dates, direction and source identifiers after your confirmation. Attachments are not imported and messages are not marked read or sent. CRM records are encrypted and associated with your Velia account. Relevant saved CRM excerpts may be processed by your selected AI model for your requests. CRM does not monitor inboxes in the background. Exported notes, reminders, calendar events, original Gmail messages and source chat messages have separate deletion controls.
  • Financial observations: balances, transactions, project names, budgets, asset holdings, dated valuations and exchange rates you provide for analysis. Relevant input is processed by your selected AI model and may remain in chat history. Bank and exchange accounts are not connected by this feature. After you confirm, Velia can keep one encrypted financial snapshot associated with your account. You can ask Velia to delete that snapshot; source messages and statements must be deleted separately from chat history.
  • Connected-service data: information retrieved from services you expressly connect, such as Google Workspace, where needed to respond to an instruction.
  • Operational information: device/session identifiers, feature use, diagnostic events, error records, security information, and usage or entitlement data necessary to run and protect the service.

3. Google Workspace access — what Velia does

Connecting Google is optional. After you grant permission using Google's authorization screen, Velia may perform the following user-directed operations within the permissions you approved:

  • Gmail: search and read relevant messages, and prepare email drafts. The current Google connection does not send emails automatically.
  • Google Drive: search for and read relevant files you ask the assistant to work with, and access files the app is authorized to manage.
  • Google Docs and Sheets: read document or spreadsheet content when supported; create documents and spreadsheets; and update spreadsheet cells at your direction.
  • Google Calendar: view events and busy time ranges, and create an event after the applicable in-app confirmation. Event creation does not automatically invite other attendees.
  • Google account identity: receive the basic identity information necessary to verify which Google account you connected.

We access Google data to fulfil the specific task you initiate. For example, if you ask Velia to summarize an email, it retrieves the relevant message and processes its contents to provide the summary. Connecting Google is not required to use Velia's general chat features.

4. Use of AI and third-party processing

To carry out an AI-assisted request, relevant excerpts of user-submitted or connected content may be transmitted to an AI inference service or other technical processor providing the requested feature. This may include selected Google message, document, spreadsheet, or calendar information relevant to your instruction. Hosting, data storage, security, and infrastructure providers may also process data to operate the service. Such processing is limited to delivering the requested service, maintaining security, or complying with legal obligations.

Google API Limited Use: Velia's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google Workspace data is not sold, shared with advertising or data brokers, used to personalize advertising, or used to train or improve general-purpose AI models. AI processing of Google data is limited to fulfilling your user-directed feature or request.

5. Where information is stored and how long

Google OAuth credentials are handled by the Velia server rather than being placed in chat messages. When you connect Google, Velia securely stores an encrypted Google access token and, if Google provides one, an encrypted refresh token in its existing database associated with your Velia account. These tokens allow Velia to maintain the authorized connection after a server restart or an access-token expiry without asking you to sign in each time. Tokens remain until you disconnect Google, revoke access, or request deletion, subject to applicable legal, security, and backup retention obligations. We use the tokens only to perform the Google actions you authorize.

Conversations and assistant responses may be saved in your Velia account history. If a reply includes information derived from a connected service, that reply may remain in conversation history until deleted. Some diagnostic, security, and backup records may remain for the periods required to operate the service, address abuse or incidents, and meet applicable obligations. We do not create a separate permanent Google Workspace archive solely from connecting your account.

6. Security

Velia uses HTTPS connections, access controls, session-bound authorization, and server-side handling of OAuth client secrets. We take reasonable measures designed to limit unauthorized access. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and deletion

  • Disconnect Google: use the connection controls in Velia. This stops further access from that connected session. You may also revoke Velia at Google Account connections.
  • Delete conversations: use the chat deletion tools in Velia; note that locally stored and account-backed conversations may have different deletion behavior.
  • Request assistance or deletion: email support@velyoncore.ai with the account and request details. Do not email passwords or access tokens. We will respond in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

8. International processing

Velia and its service providers may process information in countries different from the country where you live, subject to applicable privacy and data-protection requirements.

9. Children's privacy

Velia is a general-purpose assistant. Where the law requires consent from a parent or guardian for a minor's use of the service, that consent must be obtained.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when features or data practices change. Material changes to our use of Google user data require appropriate notice and, where required, renewed consent before a new use begins. The effective date above identifies this version.
