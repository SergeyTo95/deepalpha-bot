Content Policy

This policy applies to all VELIA features and to both free and paid accounts. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

Effective date: November 12, 2026 · Support: support@velyoncore.ai

Sexually explicit content is prohibited

You must not use VELIA to request, generate, upload, edit or distribute sexually explicit or pornographic content, including explicit depictions of sexual acts or sexualized nudity intended for sexual arousal. This prohibition applies to text, images, video and audio, including content generated by AI.

Sexual exploitation or abuse of minors, sexualized depictions of minors, and non-consensual intimate content are strictly prohibited. Do not use a real person's likeness to create sexual content or attempt to bypass content safeguards.

Non-graphic educational, medical and sexual-health discussions are permitted when they do not otherwise violate this policy or applicable law.

Other prohibited uses

Do not use VELIA for illegal activity, fraud, malware, unauthorized access, harassment, privacy violations or infringement of other people's rights.

Violations and reporting

We may reject prohibited requests, restrict access, or suspend accounts that violate this policy. Reports of prohibited content or misuse can be sent to support@velyoncore.ai. Include the relevant feature and a description of the issue; do not send illegal material or passwords.
