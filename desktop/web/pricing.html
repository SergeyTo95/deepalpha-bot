Plans and usage credits

VELIA is an AI assistant for writing, research, document creation and everyday work. Usage credits pay for eligible paid AI features inside your VELIA account.

Choose a plan in your VELIA account. Sign in and open Balance and usage to see current payment availability. Viewing this page does not create an order or charge your card.

Monthly subscriptions

Free — $0. Start with VELIA Flash. Paid subscriptions add a larger monthly usage budget:

  • Plus — $14.99/month: 1,200 credits per paid month.
  • Pro — $29.99/month: 3,000 credits per paid month.
  • Max — $59.99/month: 6,500 credits per paid month.

Subscriptions renew monthly until you cancel renewal through billing management in your account. Credits are granted after confirmed payment. Applicable taxes and the final total are shown at checkout. No unlimited usage is included.

One-time credit packages

All prices are in US dollars. These packages are one-time purchases, not recurring subscriptions. One-time card top-ups are not enabled yet. Any applicable taxes and the final total will be shown before payment.

Where to find prices in your account

Open VELIA, sign in, then open Balance and usage (Баланс и использование) in the menu. The Buy tokens (Купить токены) section shows the available packages and their prices. Subscription payment controls become available when checkout is configured. One-time card top-ups remain unavailable until their products are connected.

How credits work

Credits are internal service units, sometimes called tokens in the interface. They are not cryptocurrency, are non-transferable and cannot be redeemed for cash. Credit usage depends on the selected feature and the size of the task. A credit is not equivalent to one underlying AI model token.
