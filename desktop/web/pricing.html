Usage credit pricing

VELIA is an AI assistant for writing, research, document creation and everyday work. Usage credits pay for eligible paid AI features inside your VELIA account.

Card payments are being set up. Purchases are not enabled yet. The published package prices below are provided before launch; viewing or selecting a package does not create an order or charge your card.

Where to find prices in your account

Open VELIA, sign in, then open Balance and usage (Баланс и использование) in the menu. The Buy tokens (Купить токены) section shows the available packages and their prices. The card payment button remains unavailable until payments are enabled.

How credits work

Credits are internal service units, sometimes called tokens in the interface. They are not cryptocurrency, are non-transferable and cannot be redeemed for cash. Credit usage depends on the selected feature and the size of the task. A credit is not equivalent to one underlying AI model token.
